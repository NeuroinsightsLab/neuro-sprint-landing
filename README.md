## Problem to Solve
Traditional surveys don't reveal what consumers truly feel—95% of purchasing decisions are unconscious. Companies lose money making decisions based on subjective data. Focus groups capture what people say, not what truly appeals to them.
Our Solution
NeuroInsight Labs measures real brain and body responses to provide objective data before launching to market:
EEG: Electroencephalography (EEG)
to measure brain activity and attention
Eye-tracking: for analysis of visual attention and fixation
Biometric sensors:heart rate, skin conductance
Analysis: of involuntary emotional responses
